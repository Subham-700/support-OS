# User Personas

## Overview

SupportOS is designed for organizations that manage customer support operations. The platform supports multiple user roles, each with distinct responsibilities and permissions.

---

# 1. Organization Owner

## Purpose

The Organization Owner is responsible for creating and managing an organization within SupportOS.

## Responsibilities

- Create and configure the organization
- Manage subscription and billing
- Invite administrators and team members
- Configure organization settings
- View organization-wide analytics

## Goals

- Build an efficient support team
- Monitor customer support performance
- Ensure secure access to company data

## Permissions

- Full access to all organization resources
- Manage users and roles
- Configure AI settings
- Delete organization
- Manage billing

## Pain Points

- High support costs
- Lack of visibility into team performance
- Scaling customer support

---

# 2. Administrator

## Purpose

Administrators manage the daily configuration and operation of the support platform.

## Responsibilities

- Manage users
- Configure workflows
- Maintain the knowledge base
- Monitor system activity

## Goals

- Keep the platform organized
- Ensure accurate company knowledge
- Maintain operational efficiency

## Permissions

- Invite and remove users
- Manage roles
- Upload and update knowledge documents
- View reports
- Configure integrations

## Pain Points

- Keeping documentation up to date
- Managing growing teams
- Maintaining data consistency

---

# 3. Support Manager

## Purpose

Support Managers oversee customer support operations and ensure service quality.

## Responsibilities

- Assign tickets
- Monitor ticket queues
- Review agent performance
- Track support metrics

## Goals

- Reduce response times
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Balance agent workload

## Permissions

- View all tickets
- Assign and reassign tickets
- Generate reports
- Monitor AI performance

## Pain Points

- Uneven workload distribution
- Delayed ticket resolution
- Limited operational insights

---

# 4. Support Agent

## Purpose

Support Agents interact directly with customers and resolve support requests.

## Responsibilities

- Respond to customer inquiries
- Resolve support tickets
- Escalate complex issues
- Use AI-generated reply suggestions

## Goals

- Resolve tickets quickly
- Provide accurate information
- Maintain high customer satisfaction

## Permissions

- View assigned tickets
- Reply to customers
- Update ticket status
- Add internal notes

## Pain Points

- Repetitive questions
- Searching for company information
- Managing multiple conversations

---

# 5. Customer

## Purpose

Customers contact the organization to receive assistance.

## Responsibilities

- Create support requests
- Respond to agent messages
- Provide additional information
- Review ticket status

## Goals

- Receive quick responses
- Resolve issues efficiently
- Communicate easily with support

## Permissions

- Create tickets
- View personal tickets
- Reply to conversations
- Upload attachments

## Pain Points

- Long waiting times
- Repeating information
- Unclear ticket progress

---

# 6. AI Assistant

## Purpose

The AI Assistant helps customers and support agents by answering questions using organization-specific knowledge.

## Responsibilities

- Answer frequently asked questions
- Suggest replies to agents
- Search the knowledge base
- Summarize conversations

## Goals

- Reduce repetitive work
- Improve response quality
- Increase support efficiency

## Permissions

- Read approved knowledge base documents
- Generate reply suggestions
- Summarize tickets

## Limitations

- Cannot access another organization's data
- Cannot perform administrative actions
- Cannot answer outside approved knowledge
- Must escalate uncertain requests to human agents

---

# Summary

| Persona | Primary Responsibility |
|----------|------------------------|
| Organization Owner | Manage the organization |
| Administrator | Configure and maintain the platform |
| Support Manager | Oversee support operations |
| Support Agent | Resolve customer issues |
| Customer | Request support |
| AI Assistant | Automate and assist support interactions |
