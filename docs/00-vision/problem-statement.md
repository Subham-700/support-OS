# Problem Statement

## Background

Customer support has become increasingly complex as businesses communicate with customers through multiple channels.

Support agents spend a large amount of time answering repetitive questions instead of solving complex problems.

Examples include:

- Where is my order?
- Can I cancel my order?
- How do refunds work?
- What is the warranty period?

These repetitive tasks reduce productivity.

---

# Current Challenges

## For Customers

- Long waiting times
- Inconsistent answers
- Poor support experience

---

## For Support Agents

- Repetitive work
- Difficulty finding company policies
- Context switching between multiple tools

---

## For Managers

- Limited visibility
- Difficult to measure agent performance
- No prediction of future ticket volume

---

## For Business Owners

- Increasing support costs
- Lower customer satisfaction
- Difficulty scaling support teams

---

# Proposed Solution

SupportOS centralizes customer support into one platform.

It combines:

- Ticket Management
- AI Assistance
- Knowledge Base
- Analytics
- Workflow Automation

The AI answers common questions using company-specific documents.

Complex requests are automatically assigned to human agents.
